# Insurance-Risk-and-Claims-Analysis
Insurance Risk &amp; Claims Analysis is a data analytics project that explores car insurance policyholders, vehicle characteristics, customer demographics, and insurance claim history. The project aims to analyze claim amounts and claim frequency, identify patterns across vehicle types and customer segments ,risk factors.


## Project Overview

The Insurance Risk & Claims Analysis project focuses on analyzing car insurance policyholders, their vehicles, and their claim history. The dataset contains customer demographics, vehicle details, household income, and insurance claim information.

The main objective is to understand insurance risk factors, identify claim patterns, analyze customer segments, and generate insights that can help insurance companies improve risk assessment and pricing strategies.

## Objectives

- Analyze insurance claim amounts and claim frequency.
- Identify customer segments based on demographic characteristics.
- Study the relationship between vehicle age, car usage, and insurance claims.
- Compare claim patterns across car makes, models, and coverage zones.
- Identify high-risk customer segments.
- Create dashboards to support data-driven decision-making.

## Dataset Description

The dataset includes information about policyholders, vehicles, and insurance claims.

### Dataset Columns

| Column Name | Description |
|---|---|
| ID | Unique policyholder or insurance record identifier |
| Birthdate | Date of birth of the policyholder |
| Car Color | Exterior color of the insured car |
| Car Make | Manufacturer or brand of the car |
| Car Model | Specific model of the car |
| Car Use | Purpose of car usage, such as personal or commercial |
| Car Year | Manufacturing year of the car |
| Coverage Zone | Geographic risk area |
| Education | Highest education level attained |
| Gender | Gender of the policyholder |
| Marital Status | Marital status of the policyholder |
| Parent | Indicates whether the policyholder has children |
| Claim Amt | Total monetary value of claims filed |
| Claim Freq | Number of claims filed |
| Household Income | Annual household income |
| Kids Driving | Number of licensed drivers among the policyholder's children |

## Key Analysis Areas

1. **Customer Analysis:** Understand customer demographics and household income.
2. **Vehicle Analysis:** Examine claims by car make, model, manufacturing year, and usage.
3. **Claims Analysis:** Calculate total claim amounts, average claim amounts, and claim frequency.
4. **Risk Analysis:** Explore patterns associated with vehicle usage, coverage zones, and household driving characteristics.
5. **Customer Segmentation:** Group policyholders according to relevant demographic and risk factors.
6. **Dashboard Development:** Present key findings using charts, KPIs, and interactive dashboards.
